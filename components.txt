                         WIRING CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BATTERY → BUCK CONVERTER:
   Battery (+) → Buck IN+
   Battery (−) → Buck IN−

BATTERY → MDD20A:
   Battery (+) → MDD20A B+
   Battery (−) → MDD20A B−

BUCK CONVERTER → ESP32 + RECEIVER:
   Buck OUT+ (5V) → ESP32 VIN
   Buck OUT+ (5V) → Receiver VCC
   Buck OUT−      → ESP32 GND
   Buck OUT−      → Receiver GND

RECEIVER → ESP32:
   CH1 Signal → GPIO 18 (Steering)
   CH3 Signal → GPIO 19 (Throttle)
   CH5 Signal → GPIO 23 (Emergency Stop)

ESP32 → MDD20A:
   GPIO 25 → PWM1  (Right speed)
   GPIO 27 → DIR1  (Right direction)
   GPIO 14 → PWM2  (Left speed)
   GPIO 12 → DIR2  (Left direction)
   GND     → MDD20A GND

MDD20A → MOTORS:
   M1A, M1B → Front-right + Rear-right motors (parallel)
   M2A, M2B → Front-left  + Rear-left  motors (parallel)
