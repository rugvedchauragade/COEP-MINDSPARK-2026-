Wiring & Connections

> Based on code.ino — this is the actual firmware-verified pin mapping.

---

Power Rail


LiPo 11.1V (+) ──┬──→ MDD20A  B+
                 └──→ LM2596S IN+

LiPo 11.1V (−) ──┬──→ MDD20A  B−
                 └──→ LM2596S IN−

LM2596S OUT+ (5V) ──┬──→ ESP32    VIN
                    └──→ Receiver VCC

LM2596S OUT− (GND) ──┬──→ ESP32    GND
                     └──→ Receiver GND


> ⚠️ Set buck converter to exactly 5V with a multimeter before connecting ESP32 or receiver.

---

RC Receiver → ESP32

| Receiver Pin | ESP32 GPIO | Function |
|---|---|---|
| CH1 Signal | GPIO 18 | Steering (Left / Right) |
| CH2 Signal | GPIO 19 | Throttle (Forward / Backward) |
| VCC | VIN (5V from buck) | Power |
| GND | GND | Ground |

---

ESP32 → MDD20A

| ESP32 GPIO | MDD20A Pin | Function |
|---|---|---|
| GPIO 25 | SLEEP1 | Right channel enable (LOW = on) |
| GPIO 26 | DIR1 | Right direction |
| GPIO 27 | PWM1 | Right speed |
| GPIO 32 | SLEEP2 | Left channel enable (LOW = on) |
| GPIO 33 | DIR2 | Left direction |
| GPIO 14 | PWM2 | Left speed |
| GND | GND | Common ground |

> ⚠️ SLEEP pins are pulled LOW in firmware to enable the driver channels.
> Pulling HIGH disables them.

---

MDD20A → Motors

| Driver Terminal | Motors Connected | Side |
|---|---|---|
| M1A / M1B | Front-Right + Rear-Right (parallel) | Right |
| M2A / M2B | Front-Left + Rear-Left (parallel) | Left |

> Both motors on each side share the same PWM + DIR signal via parallel wiring.

Full Connection Summary


[FlySky FS-i6S]
      │ 2.4GHz AFHDS 2A
      ▼
[FlySky FS-iA10B Receiver]
  CH1 ──→ GPIO 18  (Steering)
  CH2 ──→ GPIO 19  (Throttle)
  VCC ──→ 5V (from buck)
  GND ──→ GND

[ESP32 DevKit V1]
  GPIO 25 ──→ MDD20A SLEEP1
  GPIO 26 ──→ MDD20A DIR1
  GPIO 27 ──→ MDD20A PWM1
  GPIO 32 ──→ MDD20A SLEEP2
  GPIO 33 ──→ MDD20A DIR2
  GPIO 14 ──→ MDD20A PWM2
  GND     ──→ MDD20A GND

[MDD20A Motor Driver]
  B+ / B− ──→ LiPo 11.1V direct
  M1A/M1B ──→ Front-Right + Rear-Right motors (parallel)
  M2A/M2B ──→ Front-Left  + Rear-Left  motors (parallel)

[LiPo 11.1V 3S]
  (+) ──→ MDD20A B+  and  LM2596S IN+
  (−) ──→ MDD20A B−  and  LM2596S IN−

[LM2596S Buck Converter]
  IN+/IN− ──→ LiPo
  OUT+ 5V ──→ ESP32 VIN  +  Receiver VCC
  OUT− GND──→ ESP32 GND  +  Receiver GND
